Know the rules

Paramedic CPD requirements in NZ, Australia, the UK and the USA

The short version of what each regulator asks for. The CPD log tracks all of it.

NZ Aotearoa New Zealand

Te Kaunihera Manapou Paramedic Council

25 hours a year from 1 April
  • 30 hours with a specialist endorsement (ECP, CCP, ICP)
  • At least 3 hours focused on culture
  • A reflection on every activity, linked to one of the five Domains
  • A selection audited after each cycle

AU Australia

Paramedicine Board of Australia (Ahpra)

30 hours 1 Dec – 30 Nov
  • At least 8 hours interactive with other practitioners
  • Portfolio of learning goals, planned activities, reflections and evidence
  • Random audits each year

UK United Kingdom

Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC)

No set hours two-year cycle
  • Meet the five Standards for CPD
  • A continuous record and a mix of learning activities
  • Show how your CPD benefits practice and service users
  • 2.5% of paramedics audited at each renewal

US United States

National Registry of EMTs (NREMT), 2025 model

60 hours every two years
  • 30 National, 15 State/Local, 15 Individual
  • Recertify by 31 March; no limit on online learning
  • State systems with their own targets: set yours in the log

Checked 31 October 2026 against the Paramedic Council, the Paramedicine Board, the HCPC and the NREMT. Rules change, and US states can add their own, so always check your regulator before you renew.

Questions

Good to know

Is this official regulator paperwork?

No. It's a set of templates to help you meet the requirements and keep good records. The Paramedic Council and the Paramedicine Board publish their own requirements, and those always come first. The pack follows them and links to them.

I don't have Microsoft Office. Will it work?

Yes. The log works in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers and LibreOffice, and the templates open in Word, Google Docs and Pages. Every template is also included as a PDF you can print.

Which countries does it work for?

New Zealand (including specialist endorsements), Australia, the UK (HCPC) and the USA. Pick where you're registered in the log and it sets the target and tracks the extras: culture hours in NZ, interactive hours in Australia, the HCPC's mix of activities and benefit to service users, and the NREMT's National, State/Local and Individual hours.

My US state doesn't use the NREMT model. Will it still work?

Yes. Choose "USA – my state / other", type in your state's hours and cycle length, and the log tracks against them. Check your state EMS office for its current rules and approved courses.

Can I use it every year?

Yes. Save a fresh copy of the log at the start of each CPD year and keep the old one as your record.

Can I share it with my team?

The pack is licensed to the person who buys it. The free quick reflection template can be shared with anyone. A team licence for a whole station or service is US$49; get in touch to arrange one.

What if it's not right for me?
